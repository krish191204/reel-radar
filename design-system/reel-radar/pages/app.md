# Reel Radar — App shell overrides

> Overrides `MASTER.md` for the Streamlit product UI.

## Page job

Help the user seed taste and get to a confident watch decision fast.

## Layout

1. Hero (brand + catalog stats)
2. Active mode panel (controls + primary CTA)
3. Ticket results grid (staggered rise)

Sidebar = taste booth (import, likes, dislikes, program).

## Notes

- Streamlit constrains component chrome; visual identity lives in `assets/theme.css`.
- Keep CTA green high-contrast on dark slate.
- Ticket cards are interaction containers (hover lift), not decorative cards for marketing.
